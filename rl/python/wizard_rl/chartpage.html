Reading the accuracy rows. Chart bid made: how often the rounded chart total was exactly the tricks the bot went on to take. Bot's own bid made: the same for the bot's real bids, which also weigh everything else it can see. Average miss: how far the chart total lands from the tricks taken, in tricks.

Everyone bids at once, so nobody's bid depends on anyone else's. A Jester is worth about zero tricks, but it is far from useless: you can always duck a trick with it, so each one you hold raises your chance of making your bid (the "Each Jester" row, compared with holding another card instead). Wizards work the same way from the other side.
